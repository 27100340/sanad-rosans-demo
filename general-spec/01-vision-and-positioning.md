# 01 — Vision and Positioning

## The problem

Private schools in Pakistan run on WhatsApp groups, paper registers, a fee spreadsheet, and one
overworked coordinator. "AI in education" products (socrat.ai, MagicSchool, Khanmigo) sell a
*chatbot for the classroom*. None of them:

1. Give the **owner / chairman / director** a single live view across branches with the ability to
   ask the school questions ("Which branch's Grade 8 Maths is slipping and why?").
2. Give **principals** real control (lock a class, approve a teacher's resource, see attendance the
   moment it is marked).
3. Make the AI tutor **obey the teacher** (their syllabus, their boundaries, their language, their
   marking scheme) rather than being a generic homework machine.
4. Serve **Islamic schools**: Hifz, Nazra, Tajweed, Tarbiyah (character), Islamic-values-aware AI.
5. Speak **Urdu** to parents and students who prefer it.
6. Work on the connectivity and devices Pakistani families actually have (phone-first, light pages).

## What Minhaj is

An **AI-native school operating system**: one system, every seat, with AI that acts differently
depending on who is sitting in the seat.

| Seat | The AI is… |
|---|---|
| Chairman / Director | an analyst that briefs them every Monday and answers questions over live data |
| Principal | a chief of staff: early warnings, teacher load, parent-issue triage |
| Teacher | a teaching assistant inside their Subject Space: lesson plans, question banks, marking, and a tutor that follows *their* rules |
| Student | a tutor that teaches Socratically within syllabus, in English or Urdu, and never just gives the answer |
| Hifz student | a patient listener that checks every word of recitation and schedules revision |
| Ustadh (Hifz teacher) | a memory-tracker that shows who is about to forget which surah |
| Parent | a daily brief, in their language, that says what happened and what to do tonight |

## Competitor gap analysis — socrat.ai

What socrat.ai does well (as of Sept 2026, from public materials): teacher-assigned Socratic dialogues,
role-play and debate activities, language practice, student transcripts visible to the teacher,
memory across sessions, K-12 age-appropriate guardrails, free tier + ~$15/mo premium + school plans.

| Capability | socrat.ai | Minhaj |
|---|---|---|
| Socratic AI dialogue on a teacher-set topic | Yes | Yes, plus grounded in the teacher's own resources and syllabus codes |
| Teacher sees transcripts | Yes | Yes, plus AI summary of misconceptions across the whole class |
| School / district admin dashboard | Minimal | Full leadership cockpit, multi-branch, ask-in-plain-language analytics |
| Multi-branch, principal per branch | No | Yes, first-class hierarchy |
| Attendance, timetable, fees, announcements | No | Yes (the school runs on it, so the AI has real data) |
| Parent portal / daily brief | No | Yes, bilingual, with "what to do tonight" |
| Marking against exam-board mark schemes | No | Yes (Cambridge-style, with evidence lines) |
| Quran Hifz recitation checking | No | Yes, word-level, with spaced revision |
| Islamic values / Tarbiyah tracking | No | Yes |
| Urdu | No | Yes for tutor, parent brief, and UI strings |
| Early-warning for at-risk students | No | Yes (attendance + marks + tutor signals) |
| Phone-first, low-bandwidth | Partial | Design goal |
| School owns its data | Vendor cloud | School-scoped tenancy; export anytime |

**Positioning line:** *socrat.ai is a tool a teacher adds to a lesson. Minhaj is the system the
school runs on, with AI in every seat.*

## Why it is lucrative

- Per-student-per-year pricing, school-wide (not per-teacher seat), so revenue scales with enrolment.
- The Hifz Engine is a defensible niche: thousands of Hifz institutions, near-zero software penetration.
- The leadership cockpit is what the *buyer* (owner) sees; the tutor is what the *user* (student) loves. Both are served.
- Bilingual, values-aware AI is a moat that Western products will not build quickly.

## Pitch narrative (10 minutes)

1. Open on the Chairman's cockpit: three branches, live. Ask: "Where are we losing students this term?"
2. Drill into a branch as the Principal: at-risk list, teacher load, one click to message parents.
3. Enter a teacher's Subject Space: resources, AI lesson plan, AI-marked assignment with mark-scheme evidence.
4. Switch to a student: the tutor refuses to hand over the answer, coaches in Urdu when asked.
5. Hifz: a child listens to an ayah, recites it back, the system highlights the two words they slipped on and schedules them for tomorrow's sabqi.
6. Parent phone view: tonight's brief.
7. Close on the "one system, every seat" slide and per-student pricing.
